# Synthesis contract

Return The Question, The Code in Question, What We Found, What We Can Reasonably Infer, Competing Hypotheses, What We Do Not Know, Sources Consulted and Confidence Summary. Include every evidence category, including empty/unavailable/inconclusive results and their reasons. Ask the parent to spot-check decisive citations against authorized live sources. If the rationale informs a change, extract Preserve / Change / Avoid / Risk constraints. Do not turn source recency or a model narrative into a fact.
