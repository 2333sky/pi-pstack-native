# Exhaust the Design Space

For a novel interaction or architecture with no strong precedent, compare meaningfully different prototypes before committing.

## Pattern

- Name the decision the prototypes must settle.
- Build 2-3 alternatives that differ in real structure or user experience, not colors/names.
- Keep prototypes cheap and isolated from production code.
- Compare them side by side against explicit criteria, then choose or reject all.

## Avoid

- Committing the first plausible shape because implementation already started.
- Three variants that share the same underlying design and therefore do not explore the space.

## Proof

Demonstrate each candidate on the real or closest available surface and record the trade-off that decided the winner.
